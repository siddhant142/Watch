# Mechanical Watch – System Design Study

![Mechanical watch system sketch](images/watch_sketch.png)

## Description
A mechanical system design project focused on understanding energy transmission,
gear ratios, tolerances, and constraint-driven decision making through the design
of a watch mechanism.

## Motivation
I built this project to move beyond theoretical mechanics and study how real-world
constraints shape small, precise mechanical systems. Watches are an ideal case
study because they combine tight spatial constraints, continuous energy flow,
and sensitivity to small design errors.

## System Overview
The project explores the watch mechanism at a conceptual and CAD level:
- Energy source and transmission concept  
- Gear train and ratio selection  
- Simplified time regulation concept  
- Housing, alignment, and tolerance considerations  

## Key Learnings
- Small tolerance errors compound rapidly in mechanical systems  
- Interfaces between components are common failure points  
- Design is largely about choosing which constraints to accept  
- Mechanical intuition improves when theory meets physical feasibility  

## Next Steps
